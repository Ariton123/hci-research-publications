# Balanced Workforce: Governance-by-Design for Privacy-Preserving Inter-Firm Workforce Leasing

## Publication

**Title:** Balanced Workforce: Governance-by-Design for Privacy-Preserving Inter-Firm Workforce Leasing

**Authors:** Melody Amaizu, Martin Stojkovski, Ariton Verush

**arXiv ID:** 2606.20732

**arXiv Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20732

**Subjects:** Software Engineering (cs.SE); Computers and Society (cs.CY)

## Background

This paper was developed from design work, documentation, and findings collected during the Design of Governance in Socio-Technical Information Systems seminar project at the University of Bern.

The work examines Balanced Workforce as a governance-by-design software architecture and socio-technical framework for privacy-preserving inter-firm workforce leasing. It connects enterprise architecture, workforce-sharing platform design, ERP and non-ERP integration, business model analysis, value exchange modeling, privacy-by-design principles, and legal, ethical, and operational risk governance.

Supporting project materials are preserved separately in the engineering portfolio under coursework/master/design-of-governance-in-socio-technical-information-systems/ to avoid duplicating the original project artifacts.
